On the bench

A full wrap, aligned and heat-set, one tumbler at a time.

  • Stainless, not plastic

    Double-wall 20 oz and 30 oz tumblers, BPA-free sliding lid. The wrap covers the body, not a small sticker patch.

  • Heat-set wrap

    Print, align, and set. Built for daily use. Avoid soaking and the dishwasher if you want the wrap to stay vivid.

  • Custom on select listings

    Meadow Speckle can take a name or short phrase. Midnight Wrap and Copper Canyon ship as designed — no text on those two.

Lead times

Handmade means a few days on the bench before it ships.

Ready-made wraps: 3–5 business days to pack, then about 2–6 days in US transit. Custom Meadow Speckle: add 2–4 business days on the press. No drop-shipping.
