On the bench

A full wrap, aligned and heat-set, one tumbler at a time.

  • Stainless, not plastic

    Double-wall 20 oz and 30 oz tumblers, BPA-free sliding lid. The wrap covers the body, not a small sticker patch.

  • Heat-set wrap

    Print, align, and set. Once engraved or personalized, wash as usual — no special care routine.

  • Personalization on select listings

    Meadow Speckle can take a name or short phrase. Midnight Wrap and Copper Canyon ship as designed. We don’t take full custom work.
